| Type | Dimensions | Orientation | Color | Use |

| **Etsy thumbnails** | 2700 × 2025 px (4:3) or square 2000×2000 | Landscape or square | **FULL COLOR** | Listing gallery |
| **Coloring pages** | 2550 × 3300 px (8.5×11 at 300DPI) | Portrait | **Black & white** | Inside PDF |

```

Generate this in tall portrait orientation, 1024×1792 pixels, black and white only.
```
